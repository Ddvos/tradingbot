# Family three is judged in money through the flip, declares its own reversal size, and is checked on the unseen period from 2025

Detector three passed family two's one out-of-sample check (#28), and ADR-0008 made the flip the price of its marks in money. The flip ran once on 22 October 2026 (#32): under the perpetual cost model 500 USD became 411 USD over the scoring period while holding made 78,000 USD; it was long 36.8% of the time, made 434 round trips, and 181 of the 868 marks it acted on were hits, 20.9%; per era it grew ×0.676, ×2.43 and ×0.501 against the benchmark's ×8.43, ×12.5 and ×1.48. The two looks pre-registered for strategy family one (#33) priced the only two position rules on the table: the undo charges 1.46 percentage points per false mark it would execute, 1.50, 1.50 and 1.33 per era, dearer than the flip's own price, and the ladder's first mark sits a median 5.8% from the extreme while leaving between 7.8% and 49.3% of the position unsold when the extreme prints. Neither showed, in any era, hits capturing more than false marks cost, and the owner closed strategy family one on 22 October 2026 with no iteration. The looks on #20 say why no position rule could: of detector three's 4,698 false marks 54.8% are pauses in a trend that went on a median 10.4% further, none of twelve price-structure conditions read at the pause lifts the share of marks at a turn past 25%, the three waits that fit inside tolerance B reach 4.2 false marks per hit at best, and the structure break, the earliest confirmation in price, sits a median 4.2% below the extreme and is within tolerance B for 19.8% of turning points. A turning point of 8% therefore costs about 9.5 points in false marks, 6.5 per hit at 1.46 points each, against a median swing of 13.8%; no rule at that scale can pay it. The record says a pause and a turn cannot be told apart at the pause, that the only thing that tells them apart is waiting, and that the price of waiting is a share of the swing, which depends on the reversal size. The reversal size was fixed at 8% in slice 2 (#11) and never revisited. Counted in hindsight over the development period on 22 October 2026, with no detector:

On 22 October 2026 the owner therefore ruled, under ADR-0004:

- **Family three's bar is money, measured through the flip.** A detector of family three passes in development when the flip on its marks grows at least as much as the benchmark in every era under the perpetual cost model: ADR-0008's bar, applied to the flip. The hit rate under tolerance B, false marks per hit, median timing and median give-up are reported for the period and per era and every pre-registration predicts them, but none is a bar. There is no cost per false mark in percentage points, because any such threshold would be read off the tables of #32 and #33, which ADR-0004 forbids. The flip stays the reference and never a candidate; it is how the bar is measured, and the strategy phase then improves on a detector whose marks already pay. The detector itself reads candles and turning points only (ADR-0003, ADR-0007); only its bar is priced on the perpetual.
- **A family declares its reversal size.** The 8% of #11 becomes family two's. Family three declares one reversal size in its pre-registration, before any run, chosen from the budget look below; the harness finds that family's turning points at that size and scores against them, and the benchmark, the eras, tolerance B and the cost models do not change. The size is a constant, not a parameter: it never follows the market, never changes between iterations, and does not change in live trading (ADR-0005). The four recorded runs stay at 8% and are not re-scored, since nothing in their definitions changes.
- **Family three's hypothesis is the retreat.** A turning point can be told from a pause only once price has retreated a set share of the reversal size from the swing extreme, and at the right reversal size that share is affordable. Iteration 1 is the simplest form: on fifteen-minute candles, as ADR-0006 set for family two, a mark on the first close that is a fixed share of the reversal size short of the swing extreme once the swing has reached the reversal size, the mirror for bottoms; the share and the reversal size are fixed from the budget look in iteration 1's pre-registration. The structure break, the close below the last higher low, is the second iteration if the first fails. This is the floor's mechanism at a scale-free distance on finer candles; the floor was kept as the hourly reference, scored 32.5% under tolerance B because a retreat can never be close to the extreme, and was never priced in money. The word confirmation is avoided for this mechanism: in the glossary a turning point is confirmed by the reversal size, and a retreat mark confirms nothing.
- **The budget look comes first, detector-free.** Before iteration 1 is pre-registered, one look over the development period, recorded with its scripts and tables on its issue: for reversal sizes 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 25 and 30%, the turning points per era and the swing sizes; perfect trading per era against the benchmark under the perpetual cost model, and the give-up budget, the largest give-up at both ends of every trade with which perfect trading still grows as much as holding; perfect long-and-short growth for information, since ADR-0001's final phase is long and short; and the retreats within swings on fifteen-minute closes, which price a retreat mark at any share: its give-up, its false marks per turning point, and what each false mark costs the flip as a single event. Perfect trading is a hindsight ceiling on turning points, the money analogue of ADR-0005's hit-rate ceilings; it involves no candidate, so it is allowed under ADR-0004 and is not the strategy run ADR-0008's definition of a look forbids. This line is not a precedent for running a candidate before its pre-registration. Choosing the size and the share from the table is a selection, and the out-of-sample check is the one test that corrects for it, as #20 recorded for its own tables.
- **The boundary moves to 2025-01-01T00:00:00Z.** The third era becomes the whole of 2022 to 2024 as ADR-0005 wrote it, and the development and scoring periods end with 2024. The two and a half months this adds were read once, as part of detector three's aggregate out-of-sample score on #28, and that caveat stands on record. Nothing from the boundary on grades anything: it is no era, no score, no look and no chart. It is the out-of-sample period, read for the first time by family three's check and by nothing before it. This spends the reservation ADR-0008 made of the stored period after the boundary for one strategy family's check; strategy family one ended without using it.
- **The check is the flip on the detector over the unseen period, once.** When a detector of family three passes in development, the store is caught up and within seven days the check runs once: the flip on the detector, backtested from the boundary to the newest stored fifteen-minute candle, the account starting flat with 500 USD at the boundary's first candle, the detector carrying its state in from the first stored candle, judged on growth at least the benchmark's over that period as one, with no eras, the benchmark buying at the period's first candle with one spot fill. The detector's own score over the period is reported next to it. The command logs the run and refuses the detector afterwards, as it refuses detector three. Paper trading is not the bar.
- **Paper trading starts now, with detector three and the flip as the reference.** A loop on live fifteen-minute spot candles from Kraken's spot endpoint runs detector three and the flip with a simulated account under the perpetual cost model, and logs Kraken Futures' mark price and real funding next to the model's fill and its 11% a year. It is never a candidate and sets no bar: it reconciles the cost model with the market before any detector of family three exists, and it is the machinery that detector runs on after its check. Hosting, alerts, the account size and the halt stay parked for the paper-trading grilling (ADR-0008).

## Considered options

- **A cost per false mark in percentage points as the bar.** Rejected: the threshold would come from the development tables, and the flip run already states the price of a detector's marks in money with no number to pick.
- **Keep 8% and design family three inside it.** Rejected on the numbers above: 9.5 points of false marks per turning point against a 13.8% swing, no condition at the pause, and 4.2 false marks per hit from the best wait.
- **A reversal size that follows the market.** Rejected: ADR-0005's reason, a fitted indicator by another name, and the turning points, and so the score, would depend on the detector's own choices.
- **A fourth era from 2025, with the boundary at the end of the fifteen-minute store, 2026-10-01.** Rejected by the owner: the stretch from 2025 stays unseen and grades nothing, so family three has a check on stored data the day its detector passes instead of waiting years for a forward period to fill.
- **A forward check in paper trading as the only check.** Rejected as the only one: at 16% or 20% a forward period needs years to hold a dozen turning points. Paper trading remains the reality check on the cost model and the road to the go-live gate.
- **Keep the boundary at 2024-10-19.** Rejected: the third era would stay cut at 18 October 2024, and the months in question are already read in aggregate.
- **Re-score the recorded runs at a new size.** Rejected: a family's definitions do not change, so there is nothing to re-score.

## Consequences

- Slice 10 teaches the harness a family's reversal size, moves the boundary, lets the backtest run the flip on any detector against that detector's recorded run, and gives the check command the flip. The budget look follows slice 10, because its third era needs the whole of 2024. Slice 11, the paper loop, depends on neither.
- Iteration 1's issue is written after the budget look, in its own grilling, with its reversal size, its share, its prediction and the bar above. The one development run is a replay plus the flip on it.
- The out-of-sample period from 2025 holds few turning points at a larger size: if the development rates hold, roughly ten to twenty at 16% to 20% in its first two years. A pass there says the detector behaved as predicted on data nobody read, not statistical proof; the account size and the drawdown halt remain the protection, as they were going to be.
- If the budget table shows that no reversal size can afford a retreat mark under long-or-flat in every era, that is the finding, and the next question is whether ADR-0001's long-and-short phase comes forward, since at larger sizes the bear markets hold the money. That question waits for the table.
- The glossary widens Reversal size, Family and Flip and gains Retreat, Perfect trading, Give-up budget, Out-of-sample period and Basis.
- ADR-0005's scoring period now ends with 2024, ADR-0007's closing clause naming a cost per false mark as family three's bar is superseded by the flip bar, and ADR-0008's reservation of the stored period after the boundary and its "checked forward" clause are superseded as above. Everything else in those decisions stands.
