# Strategies are judged like detectors, and the first trading phase is the perpetual, long or flat

Detector three passed family two's one out-of-sample check on 20 October 2026 (#28: 66 turning points, hit rate 93.9%, 11.1 false marks per hit, median give-up 0.75%), which by ADR-0002 earns the first trading phase, and ADR-0007 made that phase's backtest the place where a false mark gets its price. Before any strategy ran, the owner fixed how a strategy is judged and where it trades, on facts that involve no strategy. Counted on the recorded development run of detector three (#32), the simplest rule, all in at a bottom mark and all out at a top mark, changes position 868 times over the scoring period, 434 round trips, about 65, 58 and 22 a year in the three eras, and only 21% of the marks it acts on are hits; out of sample it changes position 66 times in 23 months, 6 of them on hits. Checked on Kraken's fee schedule on 20 October 2026, a spot round trip at the entry tier costs 1.60% in taker fees (0.80% a fill) and a perpetual round trip 0.10% (0.05% a fill); Kraken's own published funding history for its BTC perpetual, hourly and one year long, charged a long 3.3% a year, longs paying in 69% of hours. Under spot fees the simplest rule pays more than the whole account a year in fees in the first era; under the perpetual's it pays about 13% plus funding. ADR-0002 put spot first because it has no funding, no liquidation and no forced stop; re-examined, funding is small and can be modelled, a 1x long cannot be liquidated in practice, and a long-or-flat position needs no stop either, while the fee gap is sixteen to one. The owner therefore ruled:

- **The first trading phase is the perpetual, long or flat at 1x.** ADR-0002's order becomes detector, then the perpetual long or flat, then the perpetual long and short; spot drops out of the plan as a venue but stays as a cost model in every report. ADR-0001's reasoning for the short side still applies to the final phase. Detector three keeps reading spot candles; the USD-margined Kraken Futures BTC perpetual is only where the orders go.
- **Strategies follow the detector protocol.** A strategy family tests one hypothesis about how to act on marks; each iteration is pre-registered with its rule, its success criterion and a prediction, gets one development run, and a fail leads to the next iteration, never to an adjusted rerun; a family has at most five iterations and one out-of-sample check. Strategy rules are readable and read price, marks, the detector's swing extremes and the confirmed turning points only, no indicators (ADR-0003). A look may price single events on recorded marks and candles but never runs an account through a period before pre-registration.
- **The periods.** A strategy's development period is the scoring period, 2016-01-01 to the boundary, with ADR-0005's eras; the backtest starts at the first stored candle so detector three carries its state in, and the account starts at the scoring period's first candle. The stored period after the boundary serves one strategy family's out-of-sample check, with this caveat on record: detector three read it once and its aggregate score there is known, but no strategy has run there and no rule may be chosen from it. Any later strategy family is checked forward, in paper trading or against a new boundary.
- **The bar.** In each era, the account's growth after costs under the perpetual cost model is at least the benchmark's growth over that era, the benchmark buying Bitcoin once at the first candle of the scoring period with one spot fill and holding. Each era is judged on its own growth, so the position carried in from the previous era does not matter. Nothing else is a bar; the strategy's score reports, for the period and per era and under both cost models, the worst drawdown next to the benchmark's, the number of trades, the fees and funding paid, the share of time long, the result per trade grouped by the outcomes of the marks that opened and closed it, and whether a 20% drawdown halt would have fired, which it reports and never applies.
- **Two cost models**, fixed for every backtest. Every order is a market order sent at the close of the fifteen-minute candle the mark falls on and filled at the next candle's open, moved 0.05% against the bot. Spot: 0.80% taker fee a fill, no funding. Perpetual: 0.05% taker fee a fill, and funding of 0.00125% of the position's value every hour it is long, 0.01% per 8 hours or 11% a year, the industry's baseline rate and three times what Kraken charged last year, kept high on purpose because 2017 and 2021 saw far higher funding elsewhere and Kraken has no history for them. The fill price is the spot candle's; the small gap between the perpetual and spot is ignored, and paper trading checks it with real prices. Volume tiers, limit orders and the minimum order size are not modelled: a strategy that passes at the entry tier with market orders passes at anything cheaper. The account is a notional 500 USD.
- **The flip is the reference.** All in at a bottom mark when flat, all out at a top mark when long, every mark acted on. It runs once, before any candidate, outside the five iterations (#32); it is never a candidate and sets no bar, since the bars are absolute (ADR-0005); it gives the raw price of acting on every mark in money, which a detector family three would need as its bar.
- **Strategy family one's hypothesis**: position rules alone, acting on detector three's marks and reading nothing but the marks, the detector's swing extremes, the confirmed turning points and price, beat holding Bitcoin after costs in every era. Its first iteration is pre-registered after the flip's run and two recorded looks, the undo and the ladder (#33), and only if a look shows, in every era, that what the mechanism's hits would capture outweighs what its false marks would cost.
- **The live numbers wait.** Account size, risk per trade, the 20% drawdown halt, alerts, hosting and the length of paper trading, parked on 19 October 2026, are decided in the paper-trading grilling once a strategy has passed. Risk per trade does not exist for a strategy that is all in with no stop; the term returns with the short side.

## Considered options

- **Spot first, as ADR-0002 ordered.** Rejected on the numbers above: the fee alone takes more than the whole account a year under the simplest rule, and every "act on fewer marks" idea is a filter the looks on #20 showed does not exist in price structure.
- **Spot as the bar, the perpetual reported, and a move by ADR after the flip's run.** Rejected as the same decision deferred by one slice and made on a run instead of on cost facts, which ADR-0004 prefers less.
- **A real funding history as the proxy for 2016 to 2024.** Not available: Kraken's history is one year long, and the only other public history of a Bitcoin perpetual reaching back to 2016 refused the request. A constant on the high side was chosen instead; it changes by ADR with every recorded run re-scored.
- **Easier bars**, a positive result after costs or a lower worst drawdown than holding. Rejected because they pass strategies the owner would be better off not running, since holding would have paid more; the per-era bar refuses a strategy that only works in a bear market, as ADR-0005 refuses a detector that only works in one regime.
- **Pre-registering iteration 1 now**, the undo or the scale-out of #18. Rejected: one look each can price them, and the guess would spend one of five iterations.

## Consequences

- Slice 9 (#32) builds the strategy backtest with both cost models, the strategy score and the flip; the looks issue (#33) follows it; #18 waits for the ladder look.
- The glossary widens Position, Family, Look, Development period and Score to strategies and gains Flip, Growth, Worst drawdown, Fill and Cost model.
- The cost models are harness definitions under ADR-0004: they change only by ADR, on facts that involve no candidate, and every recorded backtest is then re-scored and the numbers appended to its issue.
- The live bot will read spot candles from Kraken's spot endpoint, as catch-up does, and send orders to Kraken Futures; paper trading is where that split, the basis and real funding are first seen.
- If five iterations of position rules cannot cover the price of a false mark, the finding ADR-0007 asked for is on record, and a detector family three gets its bar stated as a cost per false mark, checked forward.
